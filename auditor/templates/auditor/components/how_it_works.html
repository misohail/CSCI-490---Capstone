HOW IT WORKS

Four privacy-preserving steps

01

Analyze locally

Length, variety and estimated entropy are calculated in your browser.

02

Hash locally

Your browser creates the SHA-1 hash before the lookup.

03

Range query

Only the first five hash characters go to the HIBP range endpoint.

04

Match locally

Your browser compares the remaining hash suffix.
